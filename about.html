About us

Gear advice from people who actually keep score.

Dalton Fairway Co. exists because most golf gear reviews are written by whoever the brand shipped a free driver to. We buy what we recommend, and we say so when something isn't worth the money.

Why we started this

Golf equipment is expensive, marketing budgets are bigger than most players realize, and the gap between "tour-endorsed" and "actually helps your handicap" is wider than the industry likes to admit. We got tired of reading reviews that were really just ad copy with a star rating attached.

Dalton Fairway Co. is built around one idea: rank gear the way golf ranks itself. Every category on our Scorecard gets a Par pick (solid, budget-friendly), a Birdie pick (the value sweet spot), and an Eagle pick (no-compromises upgrade) — so you can shop for your actual game, not your ego.

How we test

We prioritize gear that fits a realistic range of swing speeds and budgets, cross-check specs (compression, shaft flex, loft angles) against what manufacturers publish, and weigh price against durability rather than just chasing the newest release. We update the Scorecard as new equipment comes out and old picks stop being good value.

How we make money

Dalton Fairway Co. is reader-supported. When you click through to a retailer and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. This is what lets us keep testing gear and publishing guides for free. It never affects which products we rank higher; our Par/Birdie/Eagle tiers are based on value and performance, not commission rate.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy, and you can reach us any time on the Contact page.
