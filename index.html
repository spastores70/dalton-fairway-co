Diamond basics

The 4Cs, in one pass.

Every diamond gets graded on these four things. Here's what actually moves the price — and what's often oversold.

Cut

Light performance

Governs sparkle. A well-cut smaller stone often reads brighter than a poorly cut larger one.

Color

Graded D–Z

Near-colorless (G–J) is where most value hides — the difference is nearly invisible set in gold.

Clarity

Internal marks

Eye-clean (VS2 and up, typically) means inclusions aren't visible without magnification.

Carat

Weight, not size

Price jumps sharply at whole and half-carat marks — shopping just under one can save real money.

Quick reference

Before you shop.

Ring sizing

Sizes shift with temperature and time of day — get measured in the evening, when fingers are at their largest.

Chain length

16" sits at the collarbone, 18" just below it, 20"+ drapes into the neckline — measure an existing piece you like.

Watch fit

Case width should roughly match wrist width in inches converted to mm — a 6.5" wrist usually suits 36–40mm.
